# Player portraits

Original club-supplied JPEG files from `SHD.rar`, copied without alteration.
The archive filenames are the confirmed shirt numbers and `data.js` maps them to the players.

The club confirmed `10.jpeg` is Tasos Babakos (#10, striker), and supplied
`6.jpeg` for Gkoumas Konstantinos in the updated archive. All 13 original
portraits are assigned. No replacement likeness or guessed identity is used.

The club separately uploaded `manousos.jpeg` for Manousos Tsaroumis (#19,
central midfielder). Its original filename and JPEG bytes are retained; this
brings the roster to fourteen players with fourteen supplied portraits.
