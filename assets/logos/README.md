# Club-supplied logos

The current crests are the ten original JPEGs uploaded in
`New folder.rar` (commit 5c268ad), copied without alteration. BLYZZ and BLUE FISH
were identified by the club. The active SHADOWS crest is `shadows-fc-2026.jpeg`.
Earlier supplied logos are retained as archive assets. `legrena.jpg` is the
original `1-Photo-1.jpg` attached separately by the club and remains in use.
The active crest appears in the header, footer, match cards, details and browser
icon. Legrena, Aston Voula, Unathletico and Savralliolid
are matched to their existing results. CSS frames Legrena's portrait canvas
without its empty top/bottom margins; the source JPEG remains unaltered.

## Presentation

Original files retain their backgrounds and internal details. At the club's
request, separate `shadows-fc-match-cutout.png`, `aston-voula-match-cutout.png`,
`unathletico-match-cutout.png` and `savralliolid-match-cutout.png` are background
removal edits made with the image editing tool. These are used only in match
cards and match details, on transparent frames. The SHADOWS edit removes black
negative space; the three opponents retain the enclosed colours of their badges.
Header, footer, browser icon, player careers, announcement photograph and all
other crests retain their existing files and presentation. No CSS background
masking or blend modes are applied.
